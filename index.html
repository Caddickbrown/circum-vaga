Who we are

Sponsored to play.
Playing to give.

Circum Vaga is a charity cricket team. Our squad shares a love of the game and a commitment to giving back. Every fixture, fundraiser and social is a chance to bring people together — and local business support makes it possible.

"Every run we score and every pound we raise is a team effort. Our sponsors are part of that team."

Our causes

Where the money goes

Every pound raised over and above our running costs goes directly to two charities that support people whose lives have been changed by injury.

🎖

Help for Heroes

Supporting wounded, injured and sick veterans and their families as they recover and rebuild their lives after service.

🏉

RFU Injured Players Foundation

Supporting rugby players at all levels who have suffered serious injuries playing the game, including spinal cord injuries.
