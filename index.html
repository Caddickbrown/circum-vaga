01 — Who we are

Cricket with a cause

Circum Vaga is a charity cricket team. We play competitive, friendly cricket and use every match as a chance to raise money and awareness for the causes we support.

The money we raise goes to charities such as Help for Heroes and The RFU Injured Players Foundation (IPF), supporting people whose lives have been changed by injury in service and in sport.

Our squad shares a love of the game and a commitment to giving back. Every fixture, fundraiser and social is a chance to bring people together, and local business support makes it possible. Sponsorship covers the cost of playing, so more of what we raise goes where it matters.

i. Visible support

Your brand is seen by players, opposition teams, spectators and our online audience throughout the season.

ii. Community goodwill

Show customers and staff that your business backs local sport and charitable fundraising.

iii. Digital reach

Recognition on the Circum Vaga website and across our social media channels.

iv. Real impact

Your sponsorship covers our running costs, freeing up more of every pound we raise for Help for Heroes and the IPF.

Every run we score and every pound we raise is a team effort. Our sponsors are part of that team.

Our causes

Where the money goes

Sponsorship covers our running costs. Everything we raise beyond that goes to two charities supporting people whose lives have been changed by injury.

In service

Help for Heroes

Supporting wounded, injured and sick veterans and their families as they recover and rebuild their lives after service.

helpforheroes.org.uk ↗
In sport

RFU Injured Players Foundation

Supporting rugby players at every level who have suffered serious injuries playing the game, including spinal cord and traumatic brain injuries.

rfuipf.org.uk ↗
